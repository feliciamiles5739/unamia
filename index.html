Measure once

Growing sizes

An age on a label is close to useless, so every card carries a height range and a chest measurement in centimetres. Measure with a soft tape and a wall: height with heels against the wall, in the evening; chest around the fullest part, tape level; back length from the neck bone to the waist.

All the growing room is in the length — four to seven centimetres at hem and sleeve, none in the width — because children go up before they go out. Between two sizes, take the larger for tees, bodysuits, pyjamas and cardigans, the smaller for shirts, the pinafore and kurtas. Wash at forty degrees, dry knits flat, never bleach the felt.

Before you send anything

Ordering and exchanges

This shop takes orders, not money: the form sends your bag as plain text, nothing is charged, and we reply by email within two working days with what is on the shelf, what needs cutting and the total with delivery. Only once you write back yes is cloth cut and the amount settled.

Courier delivery within India only: shelf pieces go out the next working day, cut pieces when finished. Exchange an unworn, unwashed, tagged piece within fifteen days; you pay return postage, we pay the outward. Pieces cut to your measurement or colour go back only if wrongly cut. No telephone orders, gift wrapping or alterations; no footwear, bags or socks.
